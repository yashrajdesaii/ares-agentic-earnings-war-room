# Skill: Earnings Memo Draft Tool

## Purpose
Generate executive-grade earnings narratives from structured analysis outputs and risk signals.

## Inputs
- Revenue variance summary
- NRR/ARR sensitivity outputs
- Headcount and operating context
- Notable risks and mitigation actions

## Workflow
1. Build memo skeleton:
   - Executive Summary
   - Performance Highlights
   - Variance Drivers
   - Risk & Mitigation
   - Next-Quarter Focus
2. Convert metrics into concise business language.
3. Ensure tone is board-appropriate and non-technical.
4. Include explicit risk statements with owner/action.
5. Produce both markdown and plain-text variants.

## Outputs
- Draft executive memo paragraph blocks
- Risk register section with severity tags
- Short IR-ready talking points

## Guardrails
- Avoid unverifiable claims.
- Distinguish facts from forward-looking commentary.
- Keep language precise, neutral, and investor-safe.
